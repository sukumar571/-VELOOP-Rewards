Objective 
Develop a completely polished Giveaway Section for VELOOP Rewards, including: 
1. Giveaway Page 
2. Giveaway Banner/Card 
3. Giveaway participation experience 
4. Giveaway status handling 
5. Winners section 
6. Previous Winners section

The final experience should make users feel: 
"This is a real, trustworthy reward opportunity, and I want to participate." 
The UI should be premium, realistic, rewarding, engaging and interactive without looking 
cheap, overly flashy, or like a casino/gambling website.
